## Verified evidence

- [UNEP, Food Waste Index 2024 press release](https://www.unep.org/en/news-and-stories/press-release/world-squanders-over-1-billion-meals-day-un-report): food service accounted for 28% of measured household, retail and food-service food waste in 2022. This establishes sector scale, not a forecast of this product's savings.
- [UNEP report](https://wedocs.unep.org/handle/20.500.11822/45230): emphasizes measurement, baselines, and reduction opportunities. Data do not establish adoption demand for Shelfday.
- [WRAP hospitality and food service](https://www.wrap.ngo/sectors/hospitality-food-service): offers free Guardians of Grub tools and promotes Target, Measure, Act. A spreadsheet or this guidance may already be sufficient for some kitchens.
- [MarketMan](https://www.marketman.com/): already provides inventory, purchasing, costing and recipes. Shelfday does not invent these capabilities; it deliberately narrows the workflow and omits integrations and procurement complexity.
- [Square restaurant inventory](https://squareup.com/ca/en/inventory-management/restaurants): existing ingredient, recipe and production tracking. Pricing and availability vary by region; no universal cost comparison is claimed.
- [GitHub contribution rules](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/reference/profile-contributions-reference): qualifying default-branch commits can count; multiple pushes do not guarantee points or additional active contribution days.

## Hypotheses to validate with operators

An owner-operated café will accept manual ingredient receipts and daily portion totals if the result helps answer what to prep and what to buy. A single-location, private workbench may be enough before POS integration. Earliest-expiry-first allocation is useful only if staff label and physically select the corresponding batch. No food-safety assurance, savings claim, demand accuracy claim, or willingness-to-pay claim has been established.

First pilot: five kitchens, seven trading days. Measure daily entry time, completeness of portion reconciliation, ingredient variance and recorded waste before deciding on POS integrations. Never infer actual waste avoided from waste merely recorded.
